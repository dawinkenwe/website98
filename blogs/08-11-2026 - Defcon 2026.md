# Defcon 2026

Defcon was so fun! This was my second year going and I had a blast. Last year my friend and I had worked on the 5n4ck3y CTF and came close to getting a prize. That CTF was presented as a text adventure game full of encrypted keys. Once you decrypted the keys, they could be entered into a customized physical vending machine. The machine would dispense receipts with NFTs that had fufrther puzzles. Or it would beep or blink at you in morse code. And if you decoded those signals, you would get a flag. Once you had 5 flags, you would be emailed a code to punch into the vending machine and it would dispense a coveted hardware badge. This badge itself would ALSO be a puzzle!

This year, we decided to start working on 5n4ck3y day one, and thanks to that timing, we were able to get our badges on the second day of the con!  The hardware challenges proved to be out of our current skillsets, but that just means we have more to learn before next year!

I am also happy to report that I able to meet my primary goal this convention! Talk to more people. Defcon is an interesting mix of folks. Cool queer people, dedicatd nerds, technology enthusiasts, and uptight department of defense folks all mingling in one space. So many people arfe learning and making cool things around you that it's hard not to be inspired to try something new.
